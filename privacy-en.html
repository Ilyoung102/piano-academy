Privacy Policy for Piano Academy

Privacy Policy — Piano Academy Effective: 2026-11-13

Operator: Uni10. Support/privacy contact: chaosryu1020r@gmail.com. No app account is required.

Microphone permission is optional and requested for pitch input. Real-time pitch is analyzed in memory. Precise conversion and imported audio analysis use temporary PCM files in the private app cache. The app deletes them after processing or cancellation and cleans up leftovers at the next launch after an unexpected stop. Raw audio is neither retained as a playable recording nor sent to developer servers. Generated note/timing events and conversion drafts may be saved locally. Capture stops in the background. Imported MIDI, MusicXML, XML, MD and supported audio files selected with the system picker are processed locally without altering the original audio. Saved scores, drafts, settings, practice history and daily free-use counters remain on the device. A short autosave delay, process termination or storage failure can lose recent edits.

Delete saved scores or discard drafts in the app. Clear Android app storage or uninstall to remove app-specific data. Original and exported files remain at their selected locations until separately deleted. Automatic app backup/device transfer is disabled. A user-selected cloud document provider or browser handles its own transfers under its own policy.

Lifetime access uses Google Play one-time billing when the store product and app billing configuration are enabled. Google handles the payment method and account; the developer does not receive card numbers. The app receives product ID, purchase state, purchase token and signed purchase data to verify entitlement and acknowledge delivery. Signed receipts are stored privately on the device and rechecked on startup/resume or restore. Offline use can retain the last verified access until the next successful check, so refunds/revocations may be delayed offline. No developer verification server is used. Clearing app data removes the local receipt cache, not Google's transaction record or purchase. Restore with the same purchase account. Seven-day passes are not currently sold. Debug preview access is separate and does not charge a payment method.

Google Play Billing, its dependencies and the Play review component are included. Google components may process purchase, service and diagnostic information under Google's policies. The app has no advertising SDK or developer-operated analytics/crash-reporting service. On-device practice/audio processing is separate from Google service processing. Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy .

Children can use the basic keyboard without a microphone or app account. A parent or guardian can help review permissions, purchases and export locations.

Support emails are deleted within one year after resolution unless a legal retention obligation or a dispute requires longer retention. Request deletion by email. The developer cannot remotely delete data stored only on your device. The mail provider's policy also applies. Changes will be reflected in the policy date and app notice; new uses requiring consent will be disclosed before use.
